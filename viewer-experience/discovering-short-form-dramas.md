# Discovering Short-Form Dramas

Short-form drama is one of ArcNova’s key content formats.

These stories are designed for fast consumption, emotional engagement, and mobile viewing. AI makes it possible to produce and test more story formats, helping creators explore new genres and styles.

Viewers can discover:

* Drama stories
* Romance stories
* Sci-fi stories
* Suspense stories
* Comedy scenes
* Fantasy content
* Character-led short videos
* Experimental AI entertainment
