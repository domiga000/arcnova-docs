# Community Layer

The community layer connects creators, viewers, and creative participants.

ArcNova’s community is designed to support:

* Creator collaboration
* Feedback loops
* Content discovery
* Creative challenges
* Community storytelling
* Education and onboarding
* Shared entertainment experiences

Community is important because AI-native entertainment is not only about tools. It is also about people creating and discovering new stories together.
