# Content Layer

The content layer includes the stories, characters, dramas, and videos created through ArcNova.

This layer is important because the long-term value of the platform depends on the quality, diversity, and repeatability of content creation.

ArcNova is designed to support many types of content, including:

* Short dramas
* Social videos
* Character stories
* AI cinematic scenes
* Brand stories
* Community-created content
* Experimental formats
