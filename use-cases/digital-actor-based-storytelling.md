# Digital Actor-Based Storytelling

Digital actors allow creators to build character-based stories without traditional production constraints.

Creators can use digital actors for:

* Recurring characters
* Fictional stories
* Short drama series
* Brand mascots
* Experimental scenes
* Character-led social videos

This helps creators develop recognizable characters and repeatable story formats.
