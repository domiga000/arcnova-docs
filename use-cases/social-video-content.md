# Social Video Content

ArcNova can support social-first video content creation.

Creators can use the platform to produce:

* Short videos
* Story clips
* Character scenes
* Promotional videos
* Trend-based content
* Creator-led entertainment clips

This is useful for creators who want to publish regularly across social platforms.
