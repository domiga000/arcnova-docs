# Token Demand Drivers

The main demand drivers for $AN include:

* Creator spending on AI production tools and services
* Token usage within ArcNova Studio workflows
* Creator incentives tied to real content revenue
* Viewer engagement that contributes to advertising revenue
* Platform growth and increased content consumption
* Expansion of AI-native entertainment use cases

The long-term goal is to align token demand with real product usage.
