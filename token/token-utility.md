# Token Utility

$AN is designed to support the following utility areas:

### 1. AI Creation Payments

Creators can use $AN to access AI-powered creation services, including video generation, AI digital actors, cinematic workflows, and production tools within ArcNova Studio.

### 2. Creator Revenue Sharing

Content created through ArcNova Studio may be eligible for revenue sharing based on platform content performance. Creators may be able to receive earnings in $AN or cash, depending on the platform’s supported withdrawal options.

### 3. Viewer Rewards

Viewers may earn $AN rewards by watching and engaging with ArcNova TV content. These rewards are designed to connect viewer attention, advertising revenue, and platform incentives.

### 4. Ecosystem Participation

$AN may be used across future ecosystem activities, premium content access, creator campaigns, community programs, and platform features.
