# ArcNova Studio

ArcNova Studio is the creation layer of the ArcNova platform.

It is where creators can generate, edit, and produce AI-powered cinematic content. ArcNova Studio is designed to simplify the creative process and help users turn story ideas into short-form dramas and video content.

ArcNova Studio supports:

* Story idea development
* AI-assisted script generation
* Character creation
* Digital actor workflows
* AI video generation
* Voice and dialogue workflows
* Scene creation
* Cinematic production workflows
* Content refinement
* Publishing preparation

The purpose of ArcNova Studio is to give creators a more complete AI production environment.

Instead of switching between many separate tools, creators can work through a connected workflow inside one product experience.
