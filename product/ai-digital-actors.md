# AI Digital Actors

AI digital actors are a key part of ArcNova’s product experience.

They allow creators to build character-driven stories without relying on traditional casting, filming, or production infrastructure. Digital actors can help creators test ideas, create recurring characters, and produce narrative content more efficiently.

AI digital actors can be used for:

* Short dramas
* Character-based stories
* Fictional scenes
* Social video content
* Brand storytelling
* Experimental entertainment formats

This feature helps reduce the gap between having a story idea and producing a finished visual scene.
