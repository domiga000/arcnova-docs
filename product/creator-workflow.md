# Creator Workflow

ArcNova’s creator workflow is designed to support the full journey from idea to publishing.

A typical workflow may include:

1. Start with an idea
   The creator enters a story concept, theme, character idea, or creative prompt.
2. Develop the story
   ArcNova helps expand the idea into a storyline, script, scene structure, or character direction.
3. Create characters
   The creator can work with AI digital actors and character tools to build the cast of the story.
4. Generate scenes
   ArcNova supports visual and cinematic content generation based on the story and characters.
5. Refine the content
   Creators can adjust the output, improve scenes, modify story elements, and prepare content for publishing.
6. Publish and distribute
   The final content can be prepared for ArcNova TV or external content channels.

This workflow helps creators move from creative concept to audience-ready content.
