# ArcNova TV

ArcNova TV is the content viewing and distribution layer of the ArcNova ecosystem.

It is designed as a destination for AI-generated short dramas, cinematic stories, and creator-led entertainment content. Through ArcNova TV, audiences can discover and watch content created with ArcNova’s AI-native tools.

ArcNova TV connects creators and viewers.

For creators, it provides a channel to publish and distribute their work.
For viewers, it provides a new way to experience short-form stories and AI-generated entertainment.

ArcNova TV helps complete the loop between creation and consumption.
