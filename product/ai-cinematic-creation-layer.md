# AI Cinematic Creation Layer

ArcNova’s AI cinematic creation layer is the foundation of the product.

It brings together different AI capabilities required for storytelling, including:

* Story generation
* Script generation
* Visual scene creation
* Digital character generation
* Voice generation
* Video generation
* Editing support
* Workflow automation

The goal is not simply to generate isolated assets. The goal is to support cinematic storytelling as a full process.

This means helping creators build characters, structure plots, generate scenes, and prepare finished short-form content.
