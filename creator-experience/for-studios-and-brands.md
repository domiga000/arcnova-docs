# For Studios and Brands

Studios and brands can use ArcNova to explore AI-powered content production.

Possible use cases include:

* Branded short-form videos
* AI-generated story campaigns
* Product storytelling
* Character-driven marketing content
* Concept testing
* Low-cost video experimentation
* Social entertainment campaigns

ArcNova can help teams test creative directions before committing to larger production investments.
