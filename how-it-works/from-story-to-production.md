# From Story to Production

Once a story direction is created, ArcNova helps move it toward production.

This may include:

* Script development
* Scene planning
* Character creation
* Dialogue generation
* Voice generation
* Visual direction
* AI video generation

The product is designed to reduce the number of tools and steps required to produce short-form cinematic content.

Instead of treating writing, casting, shooting, editing, and publishing as separate workflows, ArcNova connects them through an AI-native production process.
