# From Idea to Story

Every piece of content begins with an idea.

ArcNova helps creators transform simple ideas into structured stories. A user may begin with a genre, scene, character, mood, or short prompt. ArcNova’s AI tools can then help develop that idea into a more complete narrative direction.

Examples of starting points include:

* A romantic short drama concept
* A sci-fi character idea
* A suspense scene
* A comedy setup
* A fantasy world
* A brand story
* A social media video concept

ArcNova helps turn these starting points into usable creative material.
